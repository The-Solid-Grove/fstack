# Performance Creatives

*Distilled from course module 2.*

## Essential summary

Creative is the highest-leverage input in paid acquisition (see 2.1: with broad targeting, the creative *is* the targeting). Winning teams treat creative production as an industrialized process: a dedicated team with a clear KPI (number of top-performing creatives), weekly test-and-learn sprints, systematic idea sourcing (competitor spying, user research, iteration on winners), and constant refresh. Success is measured on CAC/ROAS and early-engagement proxies, not on aesthetics.

## Creative team and process

**KPI**: number of top-performer creatives — creatives that earn spend above a threshold ($X). Track:

- Total number of creatives produced/tested
- Success rate (share of tests that become top performers)

**Team composition**: UA manager + creative producer + designers/motion designers.

**Operating rhythm**:

- A repository of ideas and references
- A repository of finished creatives
- A Kanban board for creative production
- Weekly test-and-learn sprints
- Weekly retros and analytics on the tests
- Monthly/quarterly brainstorms and spy sessions (competitor-ad review sessions)

**Tooling**:

- Idea repository: Miro, Figma, Airtable, Google Docs, or a dedicated service like Foreplay (app.foreplay.co)
- Test retros/analytics: Google Docs, Airtable, Notion, or a dedicated service like Motion (motionapp.com)

**Creative-team analytics**:

- Monitor total creatives tested and success rate
- Track creative refresh velocity: the share of spend by creative creation date (spend should keep flowing to *new* creatives)
- Tag creatives and analyze performance by dimension: format, theme, author, etc.

> **Post-course update (verified July 2026): creative volume and win-rate benchmarks.**
> Motion's Creative Benchmarks 2026 (550k+ ads, 6,000+ advertisers, ~$1.3B
> Meta spend, Sep 2025–Jan 2026): only **~5% of ads become real winners**
> (spend ≥10x the account's median ad), roughly **half of launched ads get
> near-zero spend**, and win rates scale with account size — ~3.8% (small)
> to ~8.2% (enterprise). Top large accounts ship **~31 new ads/week** and
> mint ~6 winners/month vs ~1.75 for the average account at the same spend
> ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/thumbstop-pulse/creative-benchmarks-2026),
> [Foxwell summary](https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/motion-creative-benchmarks-2026-8-key-takeaways)).
> AppsFlyer's State of Creative Optimization 2025 (1.1M video creatives,
> $2.4B spend): **$7M+/quarter non-gaming apps run ~2,365 video variations
> per quarter** (+18% YoY), and the **top 2% of creatives absorb 43% of
> non-gaming spend**
> ([AppsFlyer](https://www.appsflyer.com/company/newsroom/pr/ai-emotion-creative-trends/)).
> Sanity-check the sprint cadence against these hit rates: at ~5%, a team
> testing under ~10 new ads a week is statistically unlikely to find even
> one winner a month.

## Metrics for evaluating creatives

| Tier | Metrics |
|---|---|
| **Primary** | CAC (the key metric); ROAS; IPM (installs per 1,000 impressions) or the web analog — paywalls per 1,000 impressions |
| **Supporting** | CTR; video hook rate (first 3 seconds); video hold rate (first 10 seconds); CPM; CPC; cost per paywall; cost per checkout |

## Generating creative ideas

### Step 1 — decide which selling points (USPs) to focus on

Ground the angle in user pains and the solutions you can offer. Sources:

1. **Spy on competitors** (see spy services below)
2. **Iterate on your current top performers**
3. **Research**:
   - Jobs-to-be-done (JTBD) studies
   - Quantitative research — including users' answers inside your quiz funnel (you can safely add extra research questions to the quiz without hurting conversion)
   - Review mining (your and competitors' reviews)
   - Search queries and user questions on forums
   - AI-assisted ideation

### Step 2 — decide how to tell the story

1. Spy on competitors' storytelling approaches
2. Cognitive biases and the psychology of attention
3. Creative methodologies and frameworks
4. Copywriting techniques

### Spy services

| Free / public | Paid |
|---|---|
| Facebook Ad Library | Adheart |
| TikTok Ad Library | SocialPeta |
| Pinterest Ad Library | AppAnnie (data.ai) |
| Google Ads Transparency Center | Apptica, Sensor Tower, etc. |

Spy service capabilities (e.g., Adheart): search by keyword, by app ID or site domain, by Facebook page. They also surface non-public pages and domains competitors run. No exact spend/volume data, but proxy metrics indicate what's running long and wide (a signal it's working).

### Recommended study material

- **Cognitive biases / attention psychology**: Robert Cialdini (*Influence*), Dan Ariely (books and YouTube talks), Daniel Kahneman (*Thinking, Fast and Slow*)
- **Copywriting**: *Cashvertising*, *Scientific Advertising* (Hopkins), Ilyakhov's *Write, Cut* (plain-language editing method)

### Creativity needs framing

Illustration: try saying every word you know, nonstop — you stall almost immediately. But if you *tell a story about something*, words come easily. The same holds for ad ideation: an unconstrained "come up with creatives" brief stalls; a focused frame (specific pain, specific USP, specific format) accelerates idea generation and removes blocks.

## Creative formats that work now

- **UGC video** — testimonials, success stories, POV clips; real people, deliberately amateur-looking footage
- **Product demonstration / unboxing**
- **Explainers and talking heads**
- **Before/after**
- **Statics** — infographics, lists, notes-app screenshots, review screenshots

> **Post-course update (verified July 2026): AI-generated creative — use it for volume, keep it human-passing.**
> Adoption is now table stakes: 8M+ advertisers use Meta's AI creative
> tools (Q1 2026 earnings; 4M at end-2024), and Meta measured a **>3%
> conversion-rate lift** for advertisers using its video-generation
> feature — real but modest, an enhancement layer rather than a substitute
> for net-new concepts
> ([PPC Land on Meta's Q1 2026 call](https://ppc.land/meta-q1-2026-56-3b-revenue-as-ai-tools-double-advertiser-adoption/)).
> Labeling: Meta auto-labels ads whose imagery was created or significantly
> edited with gen-AI with an "AI info" tag (minor retouching exempt;
> mandatory self-disclosure applies only to politics/social-issue ads) — AI
> UGC in subscription-app ads faces detection-based labeling, not a blanket
> disclosure mandate
> ([Meta Help Center](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/355108217670024/)).
> Performance: two credible studies agree AI creative works only while it
> passes as human. A TUM/Columbia/HBS working paper (16B+ impressions,
> Feb 2025) found AI ads perceived as human-made outperform on CTR, while
> glossy "AI-looking" polish (high saturation, symmetry) depresses it
> ([SSRN 5096969](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5096969));
> a Syracuse/Ipsos study (May 2026, 3,000 respondents) found human-made ads
> over-index **+11** on predicted sales impact vs **−5** for AI ads, even
> though only 13% of viewers could spot the AI
> ([EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1129874)).
> Practical rule: use AI for iteration volume and speed, keep execution
> deliberately lo-fi and human-passing, and no credible study yet shows
> disclosed-or-detectable AI creative beating human UGC.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Set the creative team's KPI as the count of top-performer creatives (spend > $X) plus success rate.
- [ ] Staff UA + creative producer + designers/motion; run weekly test-and-learn sprints with weekly retros.
- [ ] Maintain idea and creative repositories and a production Kanban board.
- [ ] Track refresh velocity (spend share by creative creation date) and tag creatives for cut-by-format/theme/author analysis.
- [ ] Judge creatives on CAC, ROAS, and paywalls-per-1,000-impressions; use hook/hold rates to diagnose video problems.
- [ ] Source angles from competitor spying, top-performer iteration, JTBD research, quiz answers, review mining, forum/search-query mining, and AI.
- [ ] Add research questions to your quiz funnel — extra questions don't drop conversion.
- [ ] Keep a rotation of UGC, demo/unboxing, explainer, before/after, and static formats in every test sprint.

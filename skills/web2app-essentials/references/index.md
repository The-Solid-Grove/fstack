# Web2Web for subscription apps

Last reviewed: September 7, 2026.

## From ad click to paid access in the app

Web2Web is a model where acquisition and purchase happen on the web, while the core product value can stay in the mobile app:

**Ad → web funnel → email → paywall → web checkout → install/open app → sign in → paid access**

This course is for founders and growth/UA leads of subscription apps with a working product and paying customers who want to open a new scalable acquisition channel.

The course is built on three layers:

1. the end-to-end mechanics of the channel, from acquisition to retention;
2. our experience building the product, the funnel, and the technical infrastructure: by June 2026 our team's product reached $2.5M annualized gross revenue, calculated as monthly revenue × 12 before fees and refunds; this is not FunnelsGrove revenue;
3. current primary documents from platforms and payment providers, together with anonymized operational lessons from real funnels.

The internal product name and confidential data are not used in this course. Numbers drawn from experience are kept clearly separate from general recommendations.

## What you will build

By the end of the course you will have:

- a decision on whether Web2Web fits your app;
- a model of CAC, pLTV, cash payback, and affordable budget;
- a map of the whole system and the owner of each part;
- a funnel brief and a screen-by-screen narrative;
- a paywall, checkout, and upsell plan;
- a design for identity, entitlement, and app handoff;
- an event taxonomy and a single acquisition-to-revenue dataset;
- an experiment backlog and a launch checklist;
- an operating system for refunds, disputes, taxes, and subscription compliance.

## How to work through the course

If Web2Web is a new channel for you, go in order. Do not start by designing a quiz or connecting Stripe: confirm fit and economics first.

To do the work properly you need a live subscription product, access to product and payment data, and someone who can build or review the backend payment and app-entitlement integration. Budget one to two working days for a first pass and for filling in the templates; build and validation take a separate cycle.

If your funnel is already running, start with the bottleneck you have:

| Symptom | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Unclear whether to launch the channel at all | [Introduction](01-what-is-web2web.md), [channel fit](02-channel-fit.md), [economics](03-economics.md) |
| People leave before the paywall | [Research and offer](05-research-and-offer.md), [funnel narrative](06-funnel-narrative.md) |
| People see the paywall but do not buy | [Paywall and checkout](07-paywall-checkout-upsells.md) |
| People pay but do not reach the app | [App handoff](08-app-handoff.md) |
| Meta delivers purchases, but the economics are unstable | [Economics](03-economics.md), [analytics](09-analytics-and-forecasting.md), [acquisition](10-acquisition-creatives.md) |
| Unclear what to test next | [Experiments](11-experimentation.md) |
| Refunds and chargebacks are rising | [Risk and compliance](12-risk-and-compliance.md) |

## The rule of this course

Web2Web is not a page between the ad and the App Store. It is a single system of acquisition, persuasion, payment, and product delivery. A local improvement only matters when it improves the final economics and the user's first useful result.

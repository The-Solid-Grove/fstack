## The BetterMe playbook (multi-funnel portfolio)

BetterMe runs a large portfolio of niche funnels, each on its own domain, all with subscriptions + intro offers + upsells, and Solidgate as the payment provider:

- **Main verticals with dedicated funnels**: Pilates (betterme-pilates.com), Fasting (betterme-fasting.com), Fitness/Calisthenics (betterme-workouts.com), Yoga (betterme-home-yoga.com).
- **Long tail of niche domains**: fasting-for-females, meal-plan, running, walking-diet, walking-plan, wall-pilates, yoga-plan, pilates-exercises, fitness-coaching, keto-diet, keto-diet-for-females, betterwoman, dancing, mind.betterme.world.

What to copy from BetterMe:

- **Heavy UA machine** — many creatives per funnel, with UTM-level funnel/creative mapping (funnel flow IDs are wired into the URL).
- **One niche = one funnel = one domain**: each audience segment gets its own tailored storyline instead of one generic funnel.
- **Full payment flow discipline**: paywall → checkout → upsells on every funnel.

## Patterns across the top funnels

Recurring mechanics that show up in nearly every strong example — treat these as a checklist for your own funnel:

1. **Storytelling-first onboarding** — the quiz builds a narrative about the user's problem before any product pitch (Mad Muscles, Simple, Noom, Nebula).
2. **Personalization as the hook** — BMI calculations, weight-loss projection graphs, language-level assessment, zodiac signs, even palm scans. Personalized output makes the paywall feel earned.
3. **Many funnels, not one** — top players run separate funnels per audience segment, goal, or season (Simple, BetterMe, Fasteasy, Nebula). Funnel count is itself a growth lever.
4. **Intro offers + upsells as the dominant monetization** — subscription with a cheap intro period, then upsells at and after checkout; pure trials are the alternative model (Noom, Nebula, Hint, Able, Virtuemap).
5. **Complete payment flow** — the strong funnels treat paywall, checkout, and upsell screens as one optimized sequence, not an afterthought.
6. **Aggressive monetization works in low-guilt niches** — fitness, fasting, and astrology funnels push hard on pricing and upsells.

> **Post-course update (verified July 2026): 2026 swipe-file additions.**
> FunnelFox's teardown of top-grossing 2026 funnels — **Babbel** (language
> learning), **Lasta Fit** (tai-chi fitness), **Flirtist** (AI dating
> assistant), plus Nebula and Fastic — confirms the patterns above still
> hold and adds mechanics worth stealing
> ([FunnelFox](https://blog.funnelfox.com/web2app-funnel-patterns-2026/),
> Jan 2026):
>
> - **Goal and context questions open the funnel** so the user feels
>   "this is for me" before any data collection.
> - **Micro-rewards between quiz steps** — examples, reassurance, and
>   social-proof interludes instead of back-to-back questions — to prevent
>   quiz fatigue.
> - **Vague intent is converted into a daily routine** — time-per-day
>   pickers, streaks, reminders, and schedules turn motivation into a
>   commitment the paid plan then "unlocks".
> - **The paywall is framed as access to a ready result** — pricing appears
>   only after the user sees a preview of their personalized plan.
> - **Urgency is always paired with risk reversal** — timers and discounts
>   run alongside a money-back guarantee (Babbel: 20-day guarantee).
> - **A trust block sits immediately before payment** — ratings, user
>   counts, security badges, FAQs (Fastic: "20M+ users, 4.8 rating";
>   Babbel: "25M subscriptions sold").
> - **Per-day price framing** on the paywall (Fastic: $0.22–$0.86 per day).
>
> Quiz-length norms from the same teardown (20–60 screens as the observed
> standard) are covered in the 3.1 onboarding update.

> **Post-course update (verified July 2026): part-2 swipe-file additions — big brands run the same playbook.**
> FunnelFox's second 2026 teardown covers **Homemade Method** (meal prep),
> **Calm** (mental health), **Flo** (period tracking), and **Runna**
> (running coaching)
> ([FunnelFox](https://blog.funnelfox.com/web2app-funnel-patterns-2026-part-2/),
> Feb 2026). What it adds to the table above:
>
> - **Established consumer brands now run quiz funnels too** — Calm and Flo
>   are top-of-category apps using the same quiz-to-paywall mechanics as
>   the web2app natives; study them as category-leader executions.
> - **Quiz length scales with vulnerability, not category size**: Calm and
>   Flo each run ~40 screens, while Runna (a task-focused training plan)
>   converts in 19 — "thirty to sixty screens are acceptable in health and
>   vulnerability-driven categories," shorter fits utility-adjacent goals.
> - **Flo's "choose-a-price" trial** — the user picks what to pay for the
>   trial (€1 or €34.99 in the observed run) — a self-serve price anchor
>   that converts price objection into a commitment choice.
> - The connecting thread across all four: the funnel "slows down" —
>   explains what's happening, helps users recognize themselves, and shows
>   personalization **before** money enters the flow.

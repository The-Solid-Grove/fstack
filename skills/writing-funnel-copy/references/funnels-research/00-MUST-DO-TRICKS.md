# MUST-DO Tricks — How to Build High-Performing Funnels

A synthesis playbook distilled from the fstack conversion framework and a step-by-step
teardown of 10 real high-performing funnels (Headway, Astroline, BetterMe Chair Yoga,
Promova, 12min, Keiki, ClaimBee, Blesse, Addmile, Monivate). Every trick below was
observed working in at least one — usually several — of those funnels. Cited examples
point to the per-funnel notes in this folder.

The single organizing principle: **a funnel is a perceived-value accumulator.** Every
screen either adds perceived value / mental fuel or spends it. The paywall converts only
if accumulated value beats the price by a wide margin. Build commitment progressively,
remove friction at payment, give hesitators a recovery path.

---

## 0. The non-negotiable spine (every funnel has these)

Across all 10 funnels the winning skeleton is identical:

```
Hook (match the ad, 1-tap entry)
  → Easy questions interleaved with reveals/validation
  → Problem activation → problem mechanism
  → Future-state projection (dated, personalized)
  → Commitment / email capture
  → Paywall (value recap + anchored price + proof + urgency + guarantee)
  → Checkout (wallet pay, in-page modal) → close-triggered downsell
  → Subscription started → upsell → web→app handoff
  → Cancellation flow WITH a save offer
```

If a section is missing, you are leaving money on the table. The teardown found most
funnels are strong through the paywall and **weak after it** (no downsell, no
cancellation save) — that is the cheapest place to win.

---

## 1. The opening (Screens 1–3): win the trust gate, spend no fuel

**MUST DO:**

1. **Match the ad in the first 3 seconds.** Screen 1 repeats the ad's exact promise.
   Headway opens *and* closes on "Become the most interesting person in the room";
   12min bookends "most interesting person at the table." Expectation match = fuel
   refill; bait-and-switch = instant exit.
2. **Make the first interaction a one-tap question, not a welcome screen.** Headway's
   first tap is age photo-cards; Addmile is a one-tap age gate. The first "yes" must
   have a ~100% agreement rate based on targeting.
3. **Hide the length at the start.** Addmile shows NO progress bar on step 1 — the bar
   appears only on step 2, after the user is already committed.
4. **Resolve threat / ally / hierarchy visually, not with claims.** High production
   value answers "are you legit," personalized content answers "are you on my side,"
   early social proof answers "are you a scam." Keiki uses a mascot (Didi) asking every
   question to read as a friendly ally and soften data collection.
5. **Promise the quiz is short.** "3-MINUTE QUIZ" (Headway, 12min) sets a small, safe
   time budget.

---

## 2. The quiz body: fuel cadence + self-generated value

**MUST DO:**

6. **Never stack 3 ask-screens in a row.** After every 2–3 questions, insert a
   give-screen: validation ("Yay, glad you're here!" — Headway), empathy ("We got
   you!" — BetterMe step-10), an insight, a stat, or a testimonial. Ability screens
   only spend fuel; motivation screens refill it.
7. **Give feedback after each answer.** Acknowledge the choice and tie it to how the
   plan adapts ("Got it — we'll shape your plan around the moments cravings hit
   hardest"). +5% CR pattern; adaptive/branched feedback +7%.
8. **Limit the first question to ≤5 options.** Reduce decision fatigue; make the first
   tap effortless. +10–15% screen CVR.
9. **Engineer self-generated value (Hitchcock) — the highest-leverage move.** Don't
   claim value; show data and let the user's brain conclude it:
   - ClaimBee shows real cited settlements ($92/device, $135M, $725M…) + a count-up to
     $1,250 framed as a *range* "depending on verifying your usage" — the user
     multiplies and owns the optimistic number.
   - Astroline has the user enter birth date/time/place and photograph their own palm,
     then reflects "their" chart back — they generated the input, so they trust the output.
   - Blesse lets users recolor and name their own prayer book before the paywall — they
     own the artifact before they pay (endowment effect).
   Self-generated expectations also protect retention: if reality disappoints, the user
   blames their own assumption, not you.
10. **Use loaders as priming real estate, not dead time.** A fake "computation" loader
    manufactures effort, personalization, and authority right before a reveal:
    ClaimBee's "Scanning FTC registry / matching open class actions"; Promova's
    "Analyzing your preferences…" ring that doubles as a 5-star testimonial carousel;
    Blesse's generation ring stopped at 78% for "almost done" tension. Add ONE micro
    yes/no question on the loader (+3% CR).
11. **Activate the problem, then reveal its mechanism, before selling the solution.**
    BetterMe step-9 makes the user admit back/knee pain, step-10 soothes it. Show *why*
    the current state happens (graph/mechanism) so the product feels like the obvious
    next step. Place social proof *before* skepticism peaks (around Q3).
12. **Climb an investment ladder.** Effort escalates: tap → trivial answers → personal
    selections → personal info → email. By the paywall, leaving feels wasteful
    (sunk cost). Astroline ends the ladder with a live palm-camera scan right before price.
13. **Use commitment locks.** A pledge / "I commit" / signature beat lifts CR +7–11%.
    Offer the explicit fork "App does it for me" vs. "I'll do it myself," then reveal the
    true effort cost of DIY (ClaimBee: "By Myself = 14 hours of work") and hand back one
    button to the product — now choosing the product is the user's own idea.

## 3. The future-state projection (just before the paywall)

This is the emotional peak and one of the two highest-impact patterns (+15% CR).

**MUST DO:**

14. **Show a dated, personalized projection chart.** Not "before vs. after" but a
    timeline anchored to today's real date:
    - Headway: growth curve "Now → FEB 26 → MAR 29, 54 books/month."
    - BetterMe: "52 kg by March 26, just in time for the Wedding" (tied to the user's
      own event).
    - Promova: "Beginner → Pre-Intermediate in 3 months."
    - Monivate: "Confident Investor by April 2026" over a rising Feb–May curve.
15. **Anchor the projection to the user's stated goal/event/date** so it reads as theirs.
16. **Stay compliant in regulated verticals** (health, finance, beauty, legal): use
    ranges, "results may vary," "consult your physician," eligibility language — never
    guaranteed outcomes. BetterMe and Keiki footnote charts as "illustrative only."

---

## 4. Email / data capture

**MUST DO:**

17. **Capture email as functionally necessary, not marketing.** Frame it as where the
    plan/results/updates are delivered. Place it late, after investment is high.
18. **Add consequence** ("we'll send your results here") so users give a real address.
19. **Validate the input** (the teardown caught several funnels shipping with no email
    validation and even dummy dev emails pre-filled — see QA checklist).

---

## 5. The paywall: value-comparison engine

The user unconsciously asks "is what I get worth more than what they ask?" Make value win.

**MUST DO (structure, in order):**

20. **Hero = personalized result summary + CTA above the fold.** Lead with the outcome
    ("Your personalized plan is ready"), show the result/projection, put a CTA above the
    fold. Astroline floats prophecy tags ("Big change at 27," "Money success at 33").
21. **Recap value** — reload every value the funnel built into short-term memory as a
    feature grid with icons, each framed as a user outcome, not a feature
    ("Never guess when to eat again," not "16/8 scheduler"). Keiki literally lists
    "No more feeling overwhelmed / more time for yourself / forget screen-time guilt."
22. **Anchor the price hard:**
    - Show 2–3 tiers; **pre-select the best-value/"Most Popular" tier** as a decoy anchor.
    - Crossed-out original price + "Save 60%" badge.
    - **Per-day reframe:** "$0.22/day" (Headway), "$0.49/day" (Promova), "$0.36/day"
      (Keiki), "$0.76/day" (Monivate). Make the number feel trivial.
    - Anchor against an external cost ("$500/yr," "one claim pays for itself").
    - For annual plans, quote per-month and pre-select annual (12min: $2.98/mo annual
      vs. $11.77/mo true monthly).
23. **Repeat the price block + CTA at least twice** and the guarantee near every CTA.
    Users decide at different scroll depths.
24. **Stack proof:** store ratings (4.8★), user counts ("150M" BetterMe, "19M" Promova,
    "5,274,333" 12min), press logos, before/after, one concrete success story
    (name → goal → result → timeframe), and vertical-specific authority (Blesse uses a
    scripture verse + clergy testimonial with handwritten signature).
25. **Honest urgency:** sticky discount + countdown banner (+10% CR). Make the discount
    feel *earned* and carried in from earlier: gamified reveal (scratch card / wheel,
    +8% CR) → pre-applied named promo code with a live timer. BetterMe "mari_feb26,"
    Keiki "JKNK_2026 — applied, 10 min left," Promova "LIMITED-SAVE-52." **The timer
    must be real and the code must actually apply through checkout.**
26. **Money-back guarantee badge beside the CTA** ("30 days, no questions") when true —
    removes the last objection (risk of loss).
27. **FAQ that secretly sells** — answer the top 3–5 barriers (How does the trial work?
    Can I cancel? Will it work for me? Scam? Refund?) while reinforcing value. ClaimBee
    answers "is this a scam?" head-on.

## 6. Checkout: kill friction at the highest-intent moment

**MUST DO:**

28. **Apple Pay / Google Pay above the card fields** (+10–15% CR). Wallet first, card below.
29. **Open checkout as an in-page modal**, not a navigation — pre-create the payment
    intent (Keiki uses a bottom-sheet with a pre-created intent).
30. **Checkout-close downsell.** If the user dismisses checkout, present one deeper,
    limited offer and keep them on the paywall to re-select (+15% ARPU). ClaimBee runs a
    real 30% → 46% Stripe-coupon ladder on abandon — most other funnels in the set
    *lack* this and lose recoverable revenue.

---

## 7. Post-purchase: upsell + handoff + retention

**MUST DO:**

31. **Immediate upsell** after "subscription started," positioned as the natural fit for
    the user's result (not a generic add-on). Use result/UTM logic to pre-select it.
32. **Engineer the web→app handoff** with a deep link + QR (Keiki) so the purchased user
    actually activates.
33. **Cancellation flow with a save offer** — pause, discount, or downgrade before a hard
    cancel. This was the most consistent gap across the teardown: nearly every funnel
    shipped only a bare "Cancel" button with zero retention attempt. Easiest win available.

## 8. Copy & attention rules (apply on every screen)

34. **Headlines ≤6 words, bold, scannable in 1 second.** If you need more words, the
    idea isn't clear yet.
35. **Body ≤2–3 short sentences;** replace text walls with an icon + a stat. Numbers beat
    paragraphs.
36. **The biggest visual element must carry the value message;** animate the
    value-carrier, keep decoration static (motion grabs attention first).
37. **3-second test per screen:** if a glance absorbs "nothing" or "a nice picture,"
    redesign. The answer must be the value point.
38. **No bold in "good-to-know" priming callouts** — bold triggers "I'm being sold to."
39. **Ranges, not promises:** "$45–$1,250" beats "$500" (less liability, users anchor high).
40. **Confident-expert tone** ("we found this for you"), never "buy now" salesperson energy.
41. **Every question must feel like it unlocks the next screen** — never ask without
    showing why the answer mattered.

## 9. Progress-bar psychology (used by nearly every funnel — use carefully)

- **Goal-gradient unlock:** Astroline frames each question as raising a "Forecast
  accuracy" gauge (34% → 67%) so answering feels like *gaining*, not *spending*.
- **Inflated denominator:** several funnels show "x / 24" or "/28" over ~14–19 real
  screens so the bar always feels nearly done. Effective, but see the QA caveat — it
  must not be so dishonest it breaks trust or contradicts visible step counts.
- **Phase labels + checkmarks** ("Profile → Patterns," hitting 100% with a checkmark)
  manufacture sunk cost before the price (12min, Monivate).

## 10. The biggest cross-funnel lessons (meta)

- **Personalization theater is common but risky.** Most of these funnels *capture*
  answers but never branch on them — the routing is strictly linear and the "personal"
  result is hard-coded. Theatrical personalization still converts IF the reflected values
  are generically true. It becomes a *bug* the moment hard-coded text contradicts the
  user's own input (Blesse's cover said "for MARI" instead of the entered name; Addmile
  hard-codes "Maria"; Keiki shows "Girl/3-4" regardless of answer). **Either branch for
  real, or keep reflected copy generic enough to always be true.**
- **Real branching is rare and is a competitive edge.** ClaimBee is the standout: genuine
  yes/no reveal branches, a married→family-claims multiplier, and an abandon-discount
  ladder. The funnel engine supports experiments/conditional edges in every project — most
  funnels simply don't use them.
- **The paywall is over-invested; the post-paywall is under-invested.** Downsell,
  upsell, and cancellation-save are the cheapest incremental revenue and the most
  frequently missing.
- **Compliance is the ceiling on the strongest lever.** The Hitchcock projection that
  converts best (ClaimBee payouts, health results, finance ROI) is also the biggest legal
  liability. Always frame as ranges + eligibility + disclaimers.

## 11. Live-funnel addendum (confirmed by walking ~24 real funnels from funnels.xlsx)

These update/extend the tricks above with what live walkthroughs proved. Full detail in
`00-LIVE-FINDINGS.md`.

42. **Ship the universal price construction:** 3 duration tiers, **3-month pre-selected**
    as "Most Popular," **per-day headline** (€0.16–0.99/day seen) over a crossed-out
    weekly/monthly anchor. This is table stakes — every live sub funnel had it.
43. **Discounted first term → full-price rollover is the monetization** (e.g. €15.19 →
    €38.95/mo; $1.98 → $28.80/mo; €1 trial → €29.99/mo). Use it — but **disclose the
    rollover clearly** (it's the #1 legal exposure; see QA B2).
44. **Always ship a checkout-close downsell** — live funnels deepen the discount on exit
    (61%→71%, →70%, $41.99→$29.99) or pre-empt it with a scratch-card. This is standard,
    not optional. (Corrects the earlier "downsell is rare" note — that was only true of
    the unwired local catalog code.)
45. **Consider the "withhold-the-result" paywall archetype** when the product's value is
    a computed result (IQ score, location, match, diagnosis): show a teaser/range, lock
    the exact answer behind a low **trip-wire** ($1.98–$1) that rolls to a high monthly.
    Add an **anti-gaming guardrail** so the result stays believable (IQ Brain rejects
    uniform answers).
46. **Personalize the promo code with the user's name/date** (`MIA_2026`,
    `Alex_15jun2026`) so the discount feels issued to them, not generic.
47. **PayPal is the floor; wallet pay is the upside.** Many top funnels ship PayPal+card
    only (Fastic, Ahead, Keiki, PawChamp, Colonbroom). Apple/Google Pay is a real +CR
    opportunity most still skip — but never ship card-only; at minimum offer one-tap PayPal.
48. **Decide web vs in-app paywall by vertical.** Self-improvement/fitness/astrology →
    web checkout. Policy-sensitive or retention-in-app verticals (AI companions, women's
    health, heart/medical) → push the paywall into the native app, use the web funnel to
    capture email/install.
49. **Vertical-specific levers to steal:** job-security FOMO + shareable certificate
    (income); pet-name + dated projection + boarding-cost anchor (pets); per-bottle
    supply-duration pricing + shipping + "secret gift" (supplements); name-on-cover
    physical product as a one-time purchase (religion); test-anxiety framing + parent-as-
    payer + per-MONTH pricing + score guarantee (edtech); fear/safety + "located but
    hidden" reveal (geo utility); vocab self-estimation + tap-and-hold promise (language);
    clinical-authority + psychometric battery + AI-therapist positioning (mental/EQ).

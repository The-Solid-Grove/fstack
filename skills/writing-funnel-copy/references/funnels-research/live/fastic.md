## Standout Techniques
- **Two long "fake-progress" loaders** bookend the funnel — the pre-quiz "Loading the quiz" and the ~100s 4-stage "Tailoring your plan" — both filled with social proof and rotating testimonials, converting wait time into trust/anticipation. Far longer/slower than Muscle Booster's.
- **Heavy product-superiority storytelling:** at least 6 side-by-side "Traditional/Restrictive vs Fastic" cards push the AI food-scanner and "enjoy food guilt-free" positioning.
- **Emotional agreement-statement series** ("My current weight makes me feel less confident", "Junk food is my guilty pleasure...") — pure System-1 confirmation + guilt buy-in with no data captured, just yes-momentum.
- **Mascot (bear) as recurring guide** softens the long funnel and humanizes section transitions.
- **Computed self-generated value:** kg-to-lose ("Losing 15 kg"), BMI ("29.0 Overweight"), BMR (1764 kcal) and macro split all derived from inputs.
- **Per-day price illusion** (€0.22–0.86/day) over crossed-out totals, with the actual €39/mo rollover in explicit (but easy-to-skim) bullets.

## Weaknesses / Risks
- **Funnel length / fatigue:** ~40+ screens plus two slow loaders is exhausting; lots of low-value interstitials (multiple near-identical AI-scanner comparison cards) risk fuel depletion before the paywall.
- **Two very long fake-progress loaders** (pre-quiz + ~100s plan build) test patience; a impatient user may abandon during "Tailoring your plan."
- **Timer credibility:** the "offer ends in 14:57" countdown is the usual evergreen-urgency pattern; refreshing/returning likely resets it.
- **Trial→rollover surprise:** €9.99 "first month" rolls to €39.00/mo; terms are disclosed but in dense bullet fine print under the CTA.
- **No Apple/Google Pay on web** reduces one-tap checkout friction relief vs Muscle Booster; relies on PayPal/card.
- **Heavy reliance on "safe weight loss" / medical framing** without clinical specificity could draw scrutiny; guarantee requires user to "demonstrate that you have followed the program," which is hard to claim in practice.

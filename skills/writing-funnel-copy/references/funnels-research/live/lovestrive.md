## Standout Techniques (vertical-unique)
- **"Love Style Test" framing** — a personality-test hook (echoes attachment-theory / love-languages pop psychology) as the lead magnet; relationship vertical leans on self-discovery curiosity rather than a problem/fear hook.
- **Authority borrowing** via press logos + "based on the latest psychological studies" to establish credibility for an emotionally sensitive purchase.
- **High-frequency social proof** ("3,318 tests taken today," 954 reviews) to normalize taking a relationship test.
- **Couple-oriented value props** (compatibility / "Understand Your Match") — distinct from solo self-improvement funnels.

## Notable Copy & Microcopy

- Block-out modal: "We're Currently Unavailable — We're not accepting new users at this time. Thank you for your interest."

## Weaknesses / Risks
- **Funnel entirely inaccessible** to new users (the core conversion path is disabled) — whether intentional A/B holdout, geo-block, or outage, it is a total revenue stop for anyone who lands and clicks.
- **Localization mismatch**: English landing copy + 100% Portuguese testimonials erodes the "verified customer" trust signal for an English-speaking visitor.
- **Weekly billing optics**: $9.99/week ≈ $43/month is materially more expensive than the $19.99/month tier, yet the weekly is the default/first card — risk of post-trial chargebacks/refund complaints (note the prominent "How to Cancel" and "Refund Policy" footer links, which usually signal heavy cancellation friction in this category).

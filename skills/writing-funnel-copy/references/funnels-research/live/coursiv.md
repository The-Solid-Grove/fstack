## Standout Techniques (vertical-unique to income / AI-upskilling)
- **Job-security / FOMO as the core emotional engine.** Unlike fitness (vanity/health), the dominant levers are *fear of being replaced* and *falling behind colleagues* — Q4 (overwhelmed), Q6 (afraid to fall behind), Q11 (career fear), plus the borrowed HBR quote. The product is sold as career insurance.
- **"Certificate / credential" as the tangible deliverable.** The whole funnel is framed around earning a *shareable AI certificate* by a concrete date (today + 28 days), giving a credential anchor that pure-skill courses lack. Before/after explicitly contrasts "No recognized credential" vs "Shareable AI credential."
- **Income/side-hustle branch** baked into goal (Q2 "Find freelance or side income") and first-week outcome (Q14 "Start AI-powered side income") — the "earn" promise.
- **Name-personalized promo code** (`Alex_15jun2026_1q3B`) — makes the 50% discount feel individually granted rather than a sitewide sale.
- **Named, modular block architecture** — each screen is a discrete reusable `block` in the URL (question-page-N, magic-page, selling-page, before-after-page), indicating a heavily A/B-tested, componentized funnel.

## Weaknesses / Risks
- **Aggressive trial-to-recurring conversion.** A €6.93–€19.99 "intro" silently rolling to €39.99 every 4 weeks is the classic complaint/chargeback pattern; the disclosure is present but in small grey legal text below the CTA.
- **Guarantee hedging.** "full refund of your initial purchase… Additional terms and conditions apply" undercuts the money-back promise's strength.
- **Timer credibility.** The countdown resets per session and the "50% off" is permanent (anchor is exactly 2×), so the urgency is synthetic — sophisticated users will distrust it.
- **Solicited/fabricated-feeling proof.** The Trustpilot card literally tagged "Solicited" and a LinkedIn-styled post mixed in can read as manufactured.
- **No per-day reframe** on this variant means the €39.99/4-weeks recurring price isn't softened — a per-week (~€10/wk) framing would likely convert better.
- **EUR pricing with "(excluding taxes)"** adds checkout sticker-shock for VAT regions.

# Paywall Blueprints

A professional paywall is a proven structure filled with your own facts. **Choose a blueprint, pick one reference paywall, copy its section order exactly, and replace every section's content.** The structure carries the persuasion; your copy carries the product; your confirmed offer facts carry the price.

Source: all 132 paywall and checkout screens in the 76 recorded funnels of the public Funnel Screens library (`fgrove references`, captured September 2026). Section orders were coded from each full-height capture's recognised text, and 16 of them were checked against image slices. Frequencies count one paywall per funnel. They describe recorded pages, not measured conversion, and reference prices, timers, reviews and claims are never evidence for your product.

## Most common order

Sticky timer/CTA bar → logo → now/goal visual or goal-date graph → "Your plan is ready" headline → answer chips → promo/offer panel → plan cards → CTA → renewal terms → payment badges → benefits → ratings/press → testimonials → FAQ → **repeat** [headline → plan cards → CTA → renewal terms] → money-back guarantee → legal footer.

Share of funnels: CTA 97%, plan cards 87%, benefits 74%, testimonials 74%, any countdown 72%, renewal terms near the CTA 71%, guarantee card 55%, ratings/awards 42%, press logos 36%, FAQ 38%, offer block repeated lower down 43%.

## Section vocabulary

| Section | Job | Content slots |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sticky bar | Keep the CTA (and any real deadline) always visible. | CTA label; real offer deadline or "Your plan is saved". |
| Logo header | Recognition. | Logo, help link. |

| CTA | Purchase action. | "Get my plan", "Start my trial". |

## B1 · Personal plan, offer first

Order (★ core):

1. ★ Sticky bar: CTA (+ real deadline only).
2. Logo header.
3. ★ Hero: now → goal visual with metric rows, **or** goal-date graph ("You can reach {goal} by {date}").
4. ★ Plan-ready headline.
5. Answer chips (2–4).
6. Promo / offer panel (optional).
7. ★ Plan cards (3; recommended middle card badged; optional bonus tag; optional one-line nudge under the cards).
8. ★ CTA.
9. ★ Renewal terms directly under the CTA (optional guarantee line between cards and CTA).
10. ★ Payment badges.
11. ★ Benefits ("What you get"), optionally after product screens.
12. ★ Ratings/awards and/or press logos.
13. ★ Testimonials or a success story with a results note.
14. FAQ (3–5).
15. ★ Repeat offer: outcome headline → chips/panel → plan cards → CTA → renewal terms → badges.
16. ★ Money-back guarantee card.
17. ★ Legal footer.

**Above the fold (874px):** sticky bar → now/goal visual → plan-ready headline → offer panel; the first card starts at the fold; the CTA stays visible.

4. ★ Benefits ("Highlights of your plan").

7. ★ Program preview (week/day grid), optional bonus.

9. ★ Offer block: plan-ready headline + chips → plan cards → guarantee line → CTA → ★ renewal terms → badges.
10. ★ Money-back guarantee → FAQ → disclaimer → support → legal footer.

## B3 · Paywall with inline payment

Order:

1. Sticky bar.
2. ★ Hero: product image, day-1 → day-30 graph or offer card.
3. ★ Plan-ready headline + 3 personalised check bullets.
4. "Choose your plan" headline (+ real deadline only).
5. ★ Plan cards (Lite / Most popular / Best price).
6. ★ CTA + guarantee line.
7. ★ Payment badges ("secure checkout").
8. Product screens or stats → ★ testimonials → CTA → community stat.
9. ★ Money-back guarantee → ★ press logos → brand promise.
10. ★ Renewal terms as bullets.
11. ★ Inline payment form (wallets, card, Subscribe).

**Above the fold:** sticky bar → hero → plan-ready bullets → first card; or the whole offer (panel, 3 cards, guarantee, CTA).

| --- | --- |
| `fgrove references step vocal-image-2ef0b5846e 37` (Vocal, 4,820px) | Offer above the fold with a personal plan header, plan preview, benefit cards that state their basis, reviews, press, ratings, FAQ, repeated cards, payment selector, plain renewal paragraph ("deleting the app does not cancel"). |

## B4 · Trial first

Order:

1. Sticky bar (optional) → logo header.
2. ★ Trial headline with the post-trial price directly under it ("Start your 7-day free trial · then €139/year").
3. Price structure: plan toggle + cards (+ trial-extension toggle), **or** "due today $0 / then $X per month".
4. ★ Benefits ("Included with your membership").
5. ★ Trial timeline: today → reminder day → charge day and amount.
6. ★ CTA ("Start free trial").
7. Inline account or payment form when checkout is on the page.
8. ★ Renewal terms with the **charge date** and renewal price/period.
9. Press, testimonials, ratings → FAQ (including "How do I cancel?") → repeat headline + CTA.

**Above the fold:** logo → trial headline → toggle → 2 cards → trial-extension toggle → sticky CTA; or headline + post-trial price → 6 benefits → CTA → timeline header.

## B5 · Compact single offer

Order:

1. Progress or logo header (or sticky bar).
2. ★ Value headline ("Unlock your full plan and get started today") + one-line outcome.
3. Goal-date graph or now → goal visual + note.
4. ★ Benefits ("Your personalised plan includes:", 5 checks).
5. ★ Plan cards (1–2, annual pre-selected; monthly equivalent + billed total), or 3 cards in "X now, then Y every N" format.
6. ★ Renewal terms (full now/then sentence).
7. Short guarantee card → testimonials / community stat.
8. ★ Sticky bottom CTA or inline CTA.
9. Separate checkout step: order summary → wallets → card → consent → "Pay and subscribe".

**Above the fold:** headline → graph → includes list → first card peeks → sticky CTA.

## Truthful replacements

Keep each structure and replace the risky device:

- **Timers (72% of recorded paywalls; resets and mismatched values observed):** use a real, fixed deadline whose expiry changes the price, a persistent non-countdown bar ("Your plan is saved — continue"), or no timer. Never reset on reload; never show two values.
- **Promo-code panel:** only for a code the visitor actually received; the same code and terms at checkout.
- **Cards:** the billed-today total is the largest number; the per-day equivalent is secondary. Inside each card: "{intro price} today, then {renewal price} every {period}". Crossed-out prices only for a price genuinely charged (usually the renewal price). Badges must be true ("Most popular" needs data).
- **Renewal terms under every CTA instance** (including repeats and inline forms): today's charge, intro length or end date, renewal price and period, how and by when to cancel, refund link; trials add the charge date and a reminder only if one is sent. Readable size and contrast, never collapsed.
- **Social proof:** real reviews with source and date; counts with basis and date; no live-purchase tickers without real data.
- **Guarantee:** headline the actual conditions ("30-day refund after completing 7 sessions").
- **Result visuals:** keep now/goal and goal-date graphs, label them illustrative, derive them from answers, keep body/health/money outcomes within typical ranges.

About 14 of 76 recorded funnels show no auto-renewal statement near the CTA; do not copy that gap. Check current requirements for the market before launch ([FBP-028](funnel-best-practices.md#fbp-028)).

## Clone procedure

1. **Pick the blueprint by fit** with [Choose a blueprint](#choose-a-blueprint): personal plan + intro pricing → B1 (B2 when the price needs a longer case); trial → B4; quiz result → B6; 1–2 plans or hosted checkout → B5; one page to payment → B3.
2. **Pick one reference paywall** from that blueprint, preferring the one whose price model matches (trial vs intro discount, 2 vs 3 plans, inline form vs separate checkout). Search more with `fgrove references list --step-type paywall --category "<category>"`.
3. **Open it completely:** `fgrove references step <funnel-id> <position>`, download the image into a new directory, view it in ~900px slices, and read the recognised text.
4. **Write the section list exactly:** order, repeated blocks and CTAs, sticky elements, card count and default selection, and what sits above the 874px fold. Record it in the arc or copy document as the paywall's skeleton.
5. **Map every section to approved facts:** plan names and durations, today's price, renewal price and period, trial, currency/tax, cancellation path, refund terms, real ratings/reviews/press with sources, user counts with basis, real program content, answers for chips and hero, legal entity.
6. **Open slots, never inventions:** `[OFFER: renewal price for 12-week plan]`, `[PROOF: App Store rating, count, date]`. A section that depends entirely on missing proof stays an open slot or is removed; never fill it with realistic-looking placeholders.
7. **Write copy for every section** in the reference's density: same section count and order, similar headline/body lengths, same number of benefits, FAQ items and testimonials. Apply [truthful replacements](#truthful-replacements).
8. **Verify:** render at mobile width; today's charge, renewal and cancellation are readable before every CTA; any deadline/code matches across instances; every number traces to an approved fact or an open slot; the section order matches the reference (record intentional differences).

Design reuses the same reference and section list ([design-funnel](../../design-funnel/SKILL.md)); implementation metadata and payment integration come from the managed project contract.

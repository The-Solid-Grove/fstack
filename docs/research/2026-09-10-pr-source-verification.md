## Corrections supported by primary sources

- **PR18 — Maine consent:** §1210-C(2) permits a checkbox, electronic signature, or other affirmative action agreeing to renewal. Replace the claim that only a dedicated checkbox/button is lawful. A dedicated control may remain a recommended design pattern. The 2025 act applies to offers or agreements entered into or renewed on or after January 1, 2026. Sources: [current statute](https://www.legislature.maine.gov/statutes/10/title10sec1210-C.html), [enacted act, §13](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/bills/getPDF.asp?item=6&paper=SP0650&snum=132).
- **PR18 — Maryland reminders:** use the statutory category of a free gift or trial lasting more than 14 days, rather than every discount period. The relevant notice window is 3–21 days before renewal; subsection (e) contains exclusions. Source: [§14-1329(c)](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Laws/StatuteText?article=gcl&enactments=false&section=14-1329).
- **PR18 — Connecticut:** the annual reminder requirement is supported. The provision requiring disclosure before a retention offer specifically concerns telephone cancellation, so presenting a line above every web save offer is a design recommendation rather than this statutory requirement. Source: [§42-158ff(d), (e)(3)(B)](https://www.cga.ct.gov/2026/sup/chap_742d.htm). The browser fetch timed out; the same official page was retrieved with curl.
- **PR18 — Virginia:** the requirement to support enrollment methods has an in-person exception; those subscriptions may be canceled electronically or by telephone. Qualify “every channel” accordingly. Source: [§59.1-207.46(B)(2), (6)](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacodeupdates/title59.1/section59.1-207.46/).
- **PR16 — creative production cost:** replace “cheapest way” with “another way to produce creative variations.” TikTok confirms Symphony Agent features and safeguards, including labels and watermarks, but the announcement supplies no comparative production-cost evidence. Source: [TikTok announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-at-cannes-lions-2026-where-creative-solutions-creator-voices-and-culture-turn-creativity-into-business-impact).
- **PR24 — Nebula outage:** remove the proposed connection between the recorded outage and an asset freeze. The FTC page supports Obrio's defendant status and lists preliminary injunctions; it provides no evidence of infrastructure shutdown causing that outage. Keep allegations identified as allegations. Source: [FTC case page](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/growthmindwisey).

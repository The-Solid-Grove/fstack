# Revenue Model Template for a Web Funnel

*Distilled from: «Revenue model for web funnel.xlsx» (course module 5). The original xlsx is the actual working template; this doc reconstructs its structure so you can rebuild or adapt it.*

## Summary

The template is a monthly cohort-based financial model for a quiz-to-paywall web funnel. It flows in one direction: **marketing assumptions → purchases → gross revenue (new + repeat, by plan) → deductions (refunds, commissions, VAT/sales tax) → net revenue → gross profit → ROAS and payback**. The engine underneath is a **subscription retention cohort table** that turns day-0 purchases into a 1-year revenue projection (LTV 1Y), which is what makes forward-looking ROAS possible. The model is maintained in two parallel views: **plan** (assumptions) and **fact** (actuals), compared month by month.

## Model structure

### 1. Marketing inputs (assumptions per source, per month)

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Marketing budget / Marketing spend | Planned and actual spend, broken out by Source 1, Source 2, Source 3, … |
| CPC | Cost per click per source |
| CR | Click → purchase conversion rate per source |
| CPA | Resulting cost per acquisition (spend ÷ purchases) |
| Purchases by source | Purchases each source delivers (budget ÷ CPC × CR, or spend ÷ CPA) |
| Purchases split / Purchases by price plan | How purchases distribute across subscription plans |

### 2. Pricing and plan mix

- Subscription plans modeled: **1 month, 3 months, 12 months**.
- Inputs: **Prices** per plan and the plan mix (% of purchases per plan), giving an **Average price**.
- Every downstream revenue line is tracked both in **$ and in %** per plan (1m / 3m / 12m) and per cohort type (new / repeat).

### 3. Subscription retention cohorts (the engine)

- A cohort table keyed by **Cohort** (acquisition month) × **Sub_month** (subscription age in renewal periods).
- Inputs: **Subscriptions count** per cohort and **Retention** (renewal rate) at each subscription month; the template also tracks **Average retention** and **average retention rate period-over-period (PoP)** to extrapolate beyond observed data.
- Output: renewal payments per calendar month per cohort → **repeat revenue** and the LTV curves.

### 4. LTV block

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| LTV 1d by price plan | Revenue per purchaser on day 0 (first payment, incl. plan mix), per plan (1m/3m/12m) and Total |
| LTV 1Y by price plan | Cumulative expected revenue per purchaser over 12 months (first payment + projected renewals), per plan and Total |
| Total LTV 1d / Total LTV 1Y | Blended values across the plan mix |

### 5. Revenue waterfall (per month)

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Gross Revenue** (before refunds, commissions, VAT) | Total billings |
| — New, $ / Repeat, $ (and %) | Split by cohort type: first purchases vs renewals |
| — By subscription plan: 1m / 3m / 12m, $ and % | Split by plan |
| Refunds | Deduction driven by the **Refund rate** assumption |
| Commissions | Payment-provider fees |
| VAT / Sales tax | Tax deducted from gross |
| **Net Revenue** (after refunds, fees, VAT) | Also expressed as **% of Gross Revenue**; split into New Net Revenue / Repeat Net Revenue |

### 6. Profit and payback

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gross profit | Net revenue − marketing spend (per month) |
| Cumulative Gross Profit | Running total — the payback line; the month it crosses zero is your break-even |
| ROAS 1d | Day-0 net revenue of a cohort ÷ its spend |
| ROAS 1Y | Projected 1-year net revenue of a cohort ÷ its spend |
| Projected Revenue 1Y / Projected Profit 1Y | Forward projection from current cohorts + retention curve |

### 7. Plan vs fact

The same structure is filled twice — planned assumptions and **fact** (actuals) — and compared per month. The forecast for the rest of a period is recomputed from actuals to date plus remaining days.

## How to use the model

1. **Seed assumptions from history.** Pull actual CPC, CR, refund rate, commissions, plan mix, and retention curves from your analytics; use them as the baseline plan.
2. **Set spend per source per month.** The model converts spend → purchases via CPC × CR (or CPA).
3. **Let cohorts compound.** Each month's new purchases enter the retention table and generate repeat revenue in later months — never model revenue as flat spend × ROAS.
4. **Deduct honestly.** Apply refund rate, payment commissions, and VAT/sales tax before calling anything "revenue"; work from Net Revenue in all profit and ROAS lines.
5. **Judge channels on ROAS 1Y, cash on Cumulative Gross Profit.** A channel can be ROAS-1Y-positive while the company is cash-negative for months; the cumulative line shows when you actually break even.
6. **Run scenarios.** Change one assumption at a time (price, plan mix, paywall CR, retention, refunds, channel budget) and read the effect on Projected Revenue/Profit 1Y — this is the point of the model: forecasting decisions, not recording history.
7. **Track plan vs fact monthly** (spend, purchases, gross/net revenue, ROAS) and re-fit assumptions whenever fact diverges.
8. **Adapt the template per project:** add platforms (web, iOS, Android), segmentation (geo, individual funnels, plans), and extra metrics as needed.

## Tips

- Keep the **new vs repeat revenue split** visible everywhere — growth driven only by new revenue with flat repeat revenue means retention is broken.
- Watch **% of Gross Revenue that survives to Net** — refunds + commissions + VAT commonly remove a double-digit share; a small refund-rate change moves profit disproportionately.
- Use **average retention PoP** to extend young cohorts' curves instead of guessing per-cohort values.
- Longer plans (12m) inflate LTV 1d but usually carry higher refund rates and lower renewal counts — model each plan's retention separately, don't average across plans.

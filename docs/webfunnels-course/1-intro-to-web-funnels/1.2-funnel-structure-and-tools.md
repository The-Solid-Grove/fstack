## Anatomy of a web2web funnel

A complete web2web funnel has five building blocks, in user-flow order:

1. **Web onboarding** — the quiz, content, or interactive functionality that qualifies and warms up the user.
2. **Sign-up** — capture the user's identity (usually email) before or around payment.
3. **Payments infrastructure** — paywall, checkout, PSP integration.
4. **User & subscription sync with the app** — the purchased subscription must carry over when the user installs the app.
5. **Tracking & analytics infrastructure** — pixels, server-side events, product analytics, A/B testing.

## Where the optimization work happens

| Area | Levers |
|---|---|
| **UA** | Creatives; channels and targeting |
| **Onboarding flow** | Quizzes and interactive functionality; personalized feedback; graphs (e.g. progress projections); loaders; branching flows |
| **Payment flow** | Pricing and discounts; trials; subscription packaging; upsells; local currencies |
| **Infrastructure** | Refunds and chargebacks; analytics; A/B tests |

## Build vs buy

| | In-house | 3rd-party tools |
|---|---|---|
| **Flexibility** | Very flexible, fully custom | Limited (UI, payment options, etc.) |
| **Time to launch** | Slow — up to ~6 months from start until metrics are trustworthy | Fast — launch within a month |
| **Cost** | Up to ~$200k including team and test traffic | ~$20–30k for the tool plus tests |

Recommendation implied by the numbers: start on a 3rd-party builder to validate the funnel economics, and only go in-house once the funnel is proven and the constraints of the tool start costing you money.

## Platform capability checklist

Whatever platform runs the funnel, the full quiz-to-paywall stack needs more
than a quiz renderer with a basic default checkout. Check for:

- **Marketing tracking:** Meta/TikTok/Pinterest pixels, server-side tracking (Meta CAPI) for signal quality on paid social, and Google Tag Manager support.
- **Product analytics:** event export to Amplitude/GA/Mixpanel, not just stored quiz responses.
- **A/B testing:** built-in split testing — funnels are improved through continuous experiments.
- **Payments:** multiple PSP integrations, a custom checkout, intro offers, and upgrades/upsells.
- **Subscription sync with the app** for the web-to-app handoff.
- **Localization:** currencies and texts.

Generic quiz/survey builders are fine for validating quiz content and lead
capture, but plan for a migration once real monetization mechanics (upsells,
intro offers, app sync, local currencies) are needed.

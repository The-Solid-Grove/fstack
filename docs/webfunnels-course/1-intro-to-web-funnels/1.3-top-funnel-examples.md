## The BetterMe playbook (multi-funnel portfolio)

BetterMe runs a large portfolio of niche funnels, each on its own domain, all with subscriptions + intro offers + upsells, and Solidgate as the payment provider:

- **Main verticals with dedicated funnels**: Pilates (betterme-pilates.com), Fasting (betterme-fasting.com), Fitness/Calisthenics (betterme-workouts.com), Yoga (betterme-home-yoga.com).
- **Long tail of niche domains**: fasting-for-females, meal-plan, running, walking-diet, walking-plan, wall-pilates, yoga-plan, pilates-exercises, fitness-coaching, keto-diet, keto-diet-for-females, betterwoman, dancing, mind.betterme.world.

What to copy from BetterMe:

- **Heavy UA machine** — many creatives per funnel, with UTM-level funnel/creative mapping (funnel flow IDs are wired into the URL).
- **One niche = one funnel = one domain**: each audience segment gets its own tailored storyline instead of one generic funnel.
- **Full payment flow discipline**: paywall → checkout → upsells on every funnel.

## Patterns across the top funnels

Recurring mechanics that show up in nearly every strong example — treat these as a checklist for your own funnel:

1. **Storytelling-first onboarding** — the quiz builds a narrative about the user's problem before any product pitch (Mad Muscles, Simple, Noom, Nebula).
2. **Personalization as the hook** — BMI calculations, weight-loss projection graphs, language-level assessment, zodiac signs, even palm scans. Personalized output makes the paywall feel earned.
3. **Many funnels, not one** — top players run separate funnels per audience segment, goal, or season (Simple, BetterMe, Fasteasy, Nebula). Funnel count is itself a growth lever.
4. **Intro offers + upsells as the dominant monetization** — subscription with a cheap intro period, then upsells at and after checkout; pure trials are the alternative model (Noom, Nebula, Hint, Able, Virtuemap).
5. **Complete payment flow** — the strong funnels treat paywall, checkout, and upsell screens as one optimized sequence, not an afterthought.
6. **Aggressive monetization works in low-guilt niches** — fitness, fasting, and astrology funnels push hard on pricing and upsells.

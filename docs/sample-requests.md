# Sample funnel requests

Use these as starting points; supply existing artifacts rather than asking the
agent to infer product or commercial facts. Adjust the requested scope and
language. These examples authorize preparation/review, not a production release.

## Strategy, Emotional Arc and design

```text
Use writing-funnel-copy, then design-funnel, for this product:

Product: [app name and official URL, plus a short factual description]
Audience: [who is deciding and the situation they are in]
Entry: [actual ad/referral promise, or say that it is not yet decided]
Offer: [confirmed free/paid access and terms, or mark them unknown]
Evidence: [product screens, sources and permitted customer proof]
Reference funnel: [URL or attached screens, if available]
Design direction: [existing Design.md and requested brand adaptation]
Language: [language for copy and client-facing materials]
Scope: complete copy plus a local clickable design; payments simulated.

First research the missing product facts and present Pre-work for my approval.
After approval, show one feeling journey and a compact Emotional Arc table:
step/job, before → after, what earns the shift, ask/barrier, answer use/next.
Explain the opening value and material branches. Wait for arc approval before
writing the screen copy.

For design, inspect the reference funnel and match source screens by job and
interaction. Show the first three actual target designs, with readable matched
reference comparisons, and wait for my feedback before extending the rest.
Keep unresolved offer facts visible and do not publish or collect payments.
```

## Redo an arc without reopening approved strategy

```text
Use writing-funnel-copy to revise the Emotional Arc in [planning document].
The approved Pre-work is [version/link]. Preserve its audience, promise and
mechanism. Use the existing product research and offer limitations.

Return one short feeling journey, one compact screen table, material branch
notes and an opening/effort review. Give every answer a named use. Show useful
value early and explain why the result and offer follow. Keep exact headlines,
questions and CTAs for after arc approval. Write the deliverable in English.
```

The [Psychology Framework](../skills/writing-funnel-copy/references/funnel-psychology-framework.md)
owns the artifact format. [design-funnel](../skills/design-funnel/SKILL.md)
owns the early visual feedback checkpoint and the rest of the design workflow.

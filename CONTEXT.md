# Glossary

- **Web-to-Web:** acquisition and purchase both happen on the web; access can be delivered in an app or browser product.
- **Course export:** a verbatim text snapshot of the canonical FunnelsGrove course at a recorded source revision.
- **Funnel QA:** evidence-backed inspection of funnel design and behavior in a specified environment and version.
- **Second-step reach:** unique first-screen visitors who view the second step, divided by unique first-screen visitors in the same cohort.
